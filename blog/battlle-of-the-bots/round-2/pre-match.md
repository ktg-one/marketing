# agents.md

## Team LLM – Definition
**Team LLM** is a modular, agentic orchestration framework created by Kev.  
It frames large language models (LLMs) as **a team of expert personas**, each with distinct roles, reasoning strengths, and workflows.  
By coordinating these models like a council of specialists, Team LLM achieves higher **breadth, depth, and reliability** than any single model.
