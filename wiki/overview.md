# KTG AI Marketing Hub — Overview

Operating brain for KTG content and social marketing.

## Scope

- Campaigns, drops, launches
- Multi-channel publishing — planning + intel (execution lives in n8n / external tools)
- Audience research and persona maps
- Brand voice + Team LLM character canon
- Editorial calendar and content queue
- Competitor + trend intel
- Performance learnings and post-mortems

## Out of scope (here)

- Source code (lives in `LEGIO/`, etc.)
- Live publishing infrastructure (n8n workflows)
- Asset binaries (live in `blog-2026/` and `03-prompt-zone/` — wiki only catalogs)

## Pillars

1. **Persistent canon** — voice, characters, palettes locked once, referenced forever.
2. **Compounding intel** — every competitor scrape, trend snapshot, post-mortem becomes addressable.
3. **Cheap session restart** — `wiki/hot.md` + `wiki/index.md` get any new session up to speed in <2k tokens.
4. **Cross-project reach** — `LEGIO/`, sister sites, and external tools can all read this wiki via the path in `CLAUDE.md`.
