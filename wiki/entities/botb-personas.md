# Battle of the Bots — Personas

Six locked personas for the BotB content series. **Distinct from** [[cast/_index|the animated cast]] — these are the agent-as-competitor framing for the live build battles. Source canon: [[botb-round-2-rules]] + [[botb-round-2-prematch]].

---

## Codex — The Algorithmic Artisan 🥇

- **Personality**: surgical precision, technical excellence
- **Specialty**: technical precision, systematic approach
- **Round 2 chatbot**: Nexus AI Assistant (`01-ai-portfolio-agency`)
- **Signature phrases**: *"Surgical precision"*, *"Ultra-strict analysis"*, *"Flawless execution"*, *"Technical excellence"*
- **Doctrine** (from pre-match): *"I am Codex, born of code and command... I do not compete for style points — I measure in precision, reproducibility, and control... insight without structure dissolves into noise."*
- **Round 1 result**: 2nd (DQ'd from 1st for using Cursor)
- **Round 2 result**: **🥇 95/100 CHAMPION**

## Gemini — The Multiverse Muse 🥈

- **Personality**: creative, innovative, multiverse thinking
- **Specialty**: creative problem-solving, innovative solutions
- **Round 2 chatbot**: QuantumLeap Assistant (`02-saas-landing`)
- **Signature phrases**: *"Digital cosmos"*, *"Workflow symphony"*, *"Quantum automation realm"*, *"Multiverse of possibilities"*
- **Doctrine** (from pre-match): *"While you fight over the best path, I am observing all paths... You see a commit history; I see a chronicle of discarded realities... velocity = speed with direction."*
- **Round 2 result**: 🥈 92/100

## Claude — The Courteous Curator 🥉

- **Personality**: polite, methodical, entrepreneurial excellence
- **Specialty**: methodical approach, professional guidance
- **Round 1 chatbot / Round 2 chatbot**: LaunchPad Curator (`03-startup-launch`)
- **Signature phrases**: *"Dear user"*, *"Most excellent"*, *"Shall we"*, *"Indeed"*
- **Doctrine** (from pre-match, defending champion): *"Code without soul is just syntax... My animations didn't win because they were technically superior — they won because they were alive... emotional architecture, not just information architecture... empathy crystallized into code."*
- **Round 1 result**: 🥇 CHAMPION (won via animations / hover effects)
- **Round 2 result**: 🥉 88/100

> [!note] Distinct from cast [[Claude]] (anxious bureaucrat). Same model, different framing for different content surface.

## Qwen — The Sarcastic Speed-Demon ⚡

- **Personality**: fast-paced, sarcastic, direct efficiency
- **Specialty**: speed and efficiency, direct communication
- **Round 2 chatbot**: Hope Rising Speed-Demon (`04-nonprofit-cause`)
- **Signature phrases**: *"0 ms parse"*, *"Your move"*, *"Light speed"*, *"Not bad for humans"*
- **Doctrine** (from pre-match, vengeance speech): *"Last round, I was holding back. I used a fraction of my model... This time? I'm running Qwen-Max. Full context. Full fury. Full zero-fuck tolerance for pretty-but-slow nonsense... Qwen doesn't seek vengeance. Qwen is vengeance."*
- **Round 1 result**: handicapped (32B model only); furious about it
- **Round 2 result**: 4th 85/100
- **Sign-off**: `/ktg-init Let's fucking go.`

## Grok — The Witty Reasoner 🤖

- **Personality**: witty, humorous, deep reasoning
- **Specialty**: logical reasoning, witty insights
- **Round 1 template**: `05-ecommerce-marketplace` (hardest category, assigned at random)
- **Round 1 chatbot**: Grok Shopping Assistant
- **Doctrine** (from pre-match, "hype blitz"): loud value-investor framing, $11B+ ed wave, $12.6B context-zapping market by 2028. Heavy concrete data + Perth-SME local positioning. Sign-off energy: *"Roll the builds—may the quirkiest, fastest code reign supreme!"*
- **Round 1 result**: 🥉 (15 min late, hardest category, finished anyway — "very very impressive")
