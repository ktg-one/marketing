# Hedra

AI lip-sync tool. Named in the [[Level-3-Production-Pipeline]] as one of two options for automated facial sync (the other being Krea.ai Live Portrait).

## Role in pipeline

Phase 4 automation glue (Automated Post-Production). Syncs character facial movements to ElevenLabs audio output, removing the need for manual frame-by-frame animation of mouths. Enables the "show don't tell" approach — characters speak without requiring hand-keyed lip animation.

## Described capability

- "Live Portrait" technology (Krea.ai variant) or Hedra's equivalent
- Automatically syncs chibi character facial expressions to generated audio
- Eliminates manual lip-sync as a bottleneck in the [[Level-3-Production-Pipeline]]

## Status in KTG production

Referenced as a strategic option — not yet confirmed as adopted in active production. The status page (`PROMPT-ZONE-STATUS.md`) is unfilled, so adoption status is unknown.
