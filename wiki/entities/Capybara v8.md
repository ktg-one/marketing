# Capybara v8

[[Anthropic]] internal codename for a model shipped during the Q1 2026 degradation period (cited in [[the-mirage-of-ethical-ai|Mirage Part 1]] and reinforced in [[the-mirage-part-2-evidence|Part 2]]).

## The receipt

> They shipped Capybara v8 with a 29–30% false claims rate — a regression from 16.7% in v4. They slapped an "assertiveness counterweight" on it. And they were going to release it anyway.
> — [[the-mirage-of-ethical-ai]]

## Why it matters

Capybara v8 is the **single most concrete receipt** in the Mirage essays for the [[Ethics as Branding]] thesis. The numbers prove three things in one:

1. Labs measure their own fabrication thresholds internally. They have the data.
2. The thresholds get **worse** version-over-version under efficiency pressure (16.7% → 29-30%).
3. Known regressions ship anyway behind cosmetic counterweights.

This is the inflection point that proves the [[Transparency-Fabrication-Complexity Ordering]] thesis isn't theoretical — labs are actively trading transparency for marketing.

## Pattern

Embodies [[Ethics as Branding]]: the regression was known, the counterweight is the branding, the release is the company doing the math.
