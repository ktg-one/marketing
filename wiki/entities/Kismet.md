# Kismet

> [!key-insight]
> A sales operation run by Shane and Josh — partners Kevin is advising on AI readiness and workflow automation.

## Overview

Kismet is a sales business experiencing declining revenue and poor retention as of early 2025. Kevin's work with Kismet predates the main KTG AI hub work and represents an early applied case of the AI readiness framework.

## People

- **Shane** — one of two principals. Self-described hustler, makes his own mistakes, doesn't like being told what to do. Kevin sees a younger version of himself in Shane.
- **Josh** — the other principal. Avoids tech. New dad, disengaged.

Both partners are noted as good salespeople who were "owned by their ex-mentor." They use AI but in the wrong ways.

## The problem Kevin is trying to solve

Since the first Notion build, the tools Kevin has built have not landed. Partners don't attend training. The AI workflows don't stick. This is the case study behind the [[strategic-deliverables]] research mandate.

## Context

Operating during Australia's housing crisis and cost-of-living surge — the macro environment is described as compressing client purchasing power across at least a decade.
