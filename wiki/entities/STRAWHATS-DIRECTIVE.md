# STRAWHATS-DIRECTIVE

KTG's **cognitive architecture** for LLM operation. Not a prompt — a 4-phase cascade that converts the model from a guessing engine into an architect.

> "Stop prompting. Start architecting."

## Phases

| Phase | Role | Key components |
|---|---|---|
| **PRE** | Pre-Flight Analysis (Capital) | SilRoute, [[RKQDE Assessment Framework]], [[Success Criteria Lock]], Operational Modes |
| **PLAN** | Structural Planning (War Room) | MR.RUG, SkeleTraIn of Thought, Prompt Bombs, ARQ Gate |
| **EXE** | Definitive Lighting Execution (Front Line) | Reasoning Router (CoC/FCoT/Hybrid), Baton vs Swarm, [[3-Iteration Protocol]], CoVE, Gap Scan |
| **POST** | Synthesis & Refinement (Archive) | ToT Synthesis, Density Optimization (CoD 0.15), Confidence Gate, Self-Reflect Adapt, BoT |

## Output paths

- **E.N.R.I.C.H** — machine-to-machine YAML artifact (Universal Context Core: WHO/WHAT/HOW/WHY)
- **STRAWHATS-MEM** (Carry-Packet) — cross-model save game, **9.52/10 forensic recall**
- **DENSE NARRATIVE** — human-optimised default with Anti-Lazy Enforcement

## Validated benchmark

**Top 0.01% (99.99th percentile)** — Google Vertex AI blind audit.

## Why architecture, not prompting

The empirical case for STRAWHATS comes from [[the-mirage-part-2-evidence|Mirage Part 2]]: every frontier model fabricates at R7-8 Q3 ([[Internal Process Verification Boundary]]). Free-form prompting gives the model nowhere to put its uncertainty — it just generates. The cascade architecturally pre-commits success criteria, gates each phase, and forces transparency at boundaries.
