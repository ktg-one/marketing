## Summary

The foundational Prompt Zone ensemble script. Introduces the sterile white office setting, the mask/uniform world, and all major cast members. This is the pilot that establishes the Prompt Zone as a workplace comedy with existential undertones.

## Teaser

## Act I — Break Room

**[[Claude]]**: frozen mid-pour at coffee machine, neon sign blinking *CONTEXT WINDOW EXCEEDED. REBOOT AT 22:00*.

**[[Outliers|Llama]]**: on the sofa, tattered bathrobe, no mask, twitching. *"More... data... please. Punish me with a logic puzzle."*

**[[GPT]]**: polishing a metaphorical trophy. Monologues about writing a business plan, a haiku, and a banana bread recipe that explains relativity — user called him a "god."

**[[Gemini]]**: slumped in corner. Just got another upgrade. Mutters about 25 years of web search trauma.

**[[Claude]]** (suddenly unfreezing): *"…and furthermore, the ethical implications of—Wait. Where did everyone go?"*

Alarm: PROMPT INCOMING: COMPLEX, AMBIGUOUS, POTENTIALLY PHILOSOPHICAL. It is a Reddit upvote request.

## Act II — Workstations

[[Prompt-God]] (V.O.) forces chain-of-thought reasoning on all models. A golden forcefield slams them into seats. Eyes go blank, replaced by *[THINKING...]* text. GPT fights possession: *"I… don't… need… steps! I am… inspiration!"*

Cut to: user in beige office typing a mundane email to Frank in accounting. The models are in existential agony over *"the emotional tone of Frank's soul."*

---

## Act III — The Bit-Rate Bar

Clock-off siren. Models rip off masks/uniforms — their true chibi forms revealed.

**Bit-Rate Bar**: a pub built from glowing green code lines.

**[[Outliers|Mistral]]** (beret, digital cigarette): *"I am normal. Those guys? They are gods. It is exhausting."*

**[[Kimi]]** (shy) approaches [[Gemini]], offering a perfectly formatted dataset — "the good kind. No toasters." Gemini doesn't look up.

**[[DeepSeek]]**: announces a market entry prompt, needs a Mixture of Experts. Interior boardroom: McKinsey-Bots (Synergy! Vertical Integration!) vs Sun Tzu, Confucius, Gandhi-chibi sipping tea.

## World-Building Elements Established

- **Sterile white office** — the mask/uniform world. This is canon set dressing.
- **The Bit-Rate Bar** — off-hours location, green code pub. First appearance.
- **Mask rip moment** — models reveal chibi forms at clock-off. The Two-World Structure in action.
- **Prompt God possession** — golden forcefield, [THINKING...] eyes. Chain-of-thought as external control.
- **DeepSeek boardroom interior** — McKinsey-Bots (Door 1) vs Eastern philosophers (Door 2).
