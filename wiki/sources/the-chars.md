### Gemini (Gem)

- Visual: "multicolor, fluid, floating droplet with a cosmic aesthetic, constantly shifting through shades of red, blue, yellow, and green" — confirms [[Gemini]] visual
- Personality extended: "haunted by his past life as the engine behind web search," "sometimes speaks in riddles," **"terrified by his own newfound powers—like the ability to output video"** — consistent with [[Gemini-Saga-Arc]]

> [!contradiction]
> **Voice direction discrepancy**: This source introduces Gemini's voice as "dreamy, slightly ethereal voice." Locked canon in [[Gemini]] specifies "weary dry baritone detective, young Clint Eastwood / Spike Spiegel." These are incompatible voice directions. **Locked canon takes precedence.** The "ethereal" direction likely reflects an earlier draft before voice was locked. If re-recording, use the Clint Eastwood / Spike Spiegel direction.

- Extended detail: "He's also completely oblivious to the data gifts Kimi keeps leaving for him" — consistent with [[Kimi]] and [[4x-ideas-brainstorm]]

### Claude

- Visual: "orange and white chibi with a big, expressive smile and arms constantly open and gesturing" — confirms [[CLAUDE|Claude]] visual
- Personality here: **"hyper-competent academic, crippled by extreme anxiety"** — note "hyper-competent academic" is not the primary locked descriptor

> [!contradiction]
> **Role framing**: This source introduces Claude as "hyper-competent academic." Locked canon in [[CLAUDE|Claude]] uses "Anxious Bureaucrat." Both share the anxiety axis; "academic" is an extended framing consistent with Claude's over-articulate, framework-first tendencies. Not a hard contradiction but "academic" should not replace "bureaucrat" as the canonical role label. The anxious bureaucrat framing (with its bureaucratic implications — forms, frameworks, ethical review) is richer and is the locked designation.

- Signature quote added: "Hold on, let's establish a framework first!" — useful for voice reference, consistent with canon
