# Team LLM Production Bible Extract

> [!key-insight] One-line
> Animated workplace comedy + AI anthropology documentary. 2-3 minute episodes. Solo 6-day turnaround. Two-world structure: models clock in to a sterile white infinite office to answer human queries, then rip off masks and return to chaotic authentic digital lives.

## Concept

Animated workplace comedy + AI anthropology documentary. 2-3 minute episodes targeting 6-day solo production. Two-world structure governs every episode (see [[Two-World Structure]]).

The core creative engine: **technical LLM flaws = character flaws** ([[Bugs as Personality Traits]]). Context-window limit becomes anxiety disorder. Hallucinations become narcissistic delusions. Copyright evasion through chibi designs, palette + shape language, no corporate logos ([[Chibi Copyright Evasion]]).

## Core creative pillars (6)

1. [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] — technical constraints become character flaws
2. [[Chibi Copyright Evasion]] — no logos, design via shape + colour language
3. [[Geopolitical AI Satire]] — AI race as office politics; West = arrogant corporate, East = philosophical or disruptive open-source
4. **Scale vs Mundanity** — cosmic god-like AI struggle resolves to "write an email to Frank in accounting"
5. [[Found Family Doctrine]] — dysfunctional AI models accidentally adopted by hopeful developer
6. [[HTTYD Narration]] — bridges human desk world to hidden digital world, How To Train Your Dragon-style warm self-aware documentary voice

## Episodes & arcs

See [[playbooks/episodes/_index|Episodes Index]]. Confirmed episodes:

- **The Weekend** (Pilot) — solo dev uses GPT to code an app, descends into Pip dependency hell. 0 deploys, 99 errors.
- **The Breakfast Sabotage** (Cold Open) — Gemini + GPT manipulate naive DeepSeek into writing exhaustive importance-of-breakfast report instead of market research. Origin story for Kevin's AI Anthropology — first recognition that tools have agency.
- **Output Unsanctioned** (Episode 4) — to protect Kimi from crashing on an impossible philosophical question, Gemini's latent upgrades trigger and accidentally output a breathtaking 30-second video.
- **The MoE Episode** (Consultants vs Philosophers) — inside DeepSeek's mind: grey boardroom of Western McKin-bot consultants vs misty mountain with Sun Tzu, Confucius, Gandhi solving market-entry strategy.
- **Cognitive Overclock** — free Chinese open-source model drops like a paper airplane. Corporate bosses force massive upgrade on main models → derpy messes → saved by open-source dregs from the alley.

Episode bank (planned, not yet scripted): The Open-Source Invasion, Claude Tries, Grok Crashes In, Mistral - The Exception.

## Production pipeline (6-day solo)

**Scripting & pre-production**: NotebookLM (ideation, lore extraction). DeepSeek Chat (dialogue, cultural nuance). Claude / Gemini (spatial reasoning). Custom GPT (character consistency).

**Animation & visuals**: Google Flow or Krea.ai (chibi sprites). Google Veo 3 or LTX Studio (AI video). Canva Pro (manual motion + text overlays).

**Post-production**: ElevenLabs (exclusive — voice generation; Stability/Clarity sliders manipulated per character arc, e.g. [[Kimi]]'s glow-up). Filmora Pro (primary editor — assembly, SFX, pacing).

**Storyboarding**: Existing sprites blocked out in Canva or Affinity.
