1. **Humans & AI as Co-Evolutionary Partners** — AI is not just a tool but a cultural and evolutionary partner
2. **Civilization Accelerationism** — Orchestrated AI helps society break stagnation cycles

4. **Ethics & Empathy** — Orchestration guided by altruism, compassion, justice
