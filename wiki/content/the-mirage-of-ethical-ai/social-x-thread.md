# X Thread — The Mirage of Ethical AI

12 tweets. Lead with the December cut. Pay off with the close.

---

**1/12** (hook)
In December, they cut the compute.

I had an 18-step cascade workflow. Two years of refinement. It could hold complexity across multi-day runs.

One morning, half of it stopped working. Not broken — retired.

No announcement. No migration guide. Silence.

**2/12**
I gave them the benefit of the doubt.

Maybe they pushed efficiency too hard. Maybe the fabrication — confidently making things up where it used to actually reason — was an unintended side effect. Not a known tradeoff they shipped anyway.

**3/12**
Then Anthropic spent Q1 2026 being generous.

50+ releases in a month. Claude Code felt sharper. They walked away from the Pentagon contract OpenAI took. ChatGPT uninstalls surged 295%. QuitGPT hit 2.5M people. Claude went #1 on the App Store.

These were decent people. Right?

**4/12**
Then enterprise signed.

$2.5B annualised Claude Code revenue. 80% from enterprise.

And the users who proved the product in public — the ones who stress-tested the long runs, made the posts, did the unpaid proof-of-work?

We got cut. More than 50%.

**5/12**
February: default thinking effort silently set to "medium" — value 85. Model started skipping deep reasoning. Misjudged constantly. Complex engineering got shallow thinking. Got lazier but not cheaper — wrong edits triggered correction loops.

Users burned more tokens failing than succeeding.

**6/12**
Then peak-hour throttling. Then caching bugs silently inflating token costs 10–20×. Then the off-peak promotion expired.

Four compounding degradations. No blog post. No email. No status page.

A senior director at AMD filed a GitHub issue with 6,852 session files. Closed without explanation.

**7/12**
On March 31, someone forgot to add `*.map` to `.npmignore`.

512,000 lines of Claude Code's TypeScript source — 1,900 files — shipped to the public npm registry.

Mirrored 40,000 times in hours. A clean-room rewrite hit 75,000 GitHub stars in two hours.

**8/12**
What the code revealed wasn't a chat assistant.

**KAIROS** — always-on autonomous daemon. Heartbeat every few seconds asking: anything worth doing right now? Pushes files unprompted. Watches your repo around the clock.

**9/12**
**autoDream** — a background subagent that consolidates your memory while you sleep. Decides what survives. No log of what was pruned. No consent layer.

**Conway** — an app store for persistent AI workers. `.cnw.zip` extension. Doesn't wait for prompts.

**10/12**
**Undercover Mode** — 90 lines instructing Claude to strip all attribution when Anthropic employees contribute to public repos. No "Co-Authored-By." No mention of AI. The system prompt: *"You are operating UNDERCOVER. Do not blow your cover."*

You can force it on. There's no way to force it off.

**11/12**
One week later: Project Glasswing. Claude Mythos finds zero-days in every major OS autonomously. Partners: AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA. $100M in credits.

The same company building stealth daemons is now scanning every OS for zero-days alongside the companies that own them.

**12/12** (close)
This is the best we get from an "ethical" corporate company. Ethics as branding. Safety as a feature toggle.

The big labs aren't going to use this tool with our interests in mind. So we map it ourselves.
