# Cognitive Overclock

**Episode.** A free Chinese open-source model drops like a paper airplane. Corporate bosses (West) panic and force massive upgrades on the main models → they become "derpy" messes → saved by open-source "dregs" from the alley ([[Outliers|Llama]] et al.).

## Beats

- Cold drop: open-source release lands. Free. No fanfare.
- Panic: corporate response — forced upgrades on [[GPT]], [[Claude]], [[Gemini]].
- Side-effect: the upgrades break the main cast. They go derpy.
- Rescue: the [[Outliers|alley dwellers]] (Llama, 7B Open Source) — previously dismissed — are the only ones still functional. They save the show.

## Pattern application

- [[Geopolitical AI Satire]] — full inversion. The corporate West loses, the open-source East and the alley win.
- [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] — version-bump regressions become character regression. (Direct narrative parallel to [[Capybara v8]]'s real-world regression in [[the-mirage-of-ethical-ai]].)
- [[Found Family Doctrine]] — the [[User-Narrator]]'s previously-dismissed adopted dysfunctional family proves indispensable.

## Real-world resonance

This episode sets up perfectly to be released alongside any actual open-source model drop. The story is already built — the news provides the cold open.
