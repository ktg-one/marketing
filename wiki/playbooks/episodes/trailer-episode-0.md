# The Prompt Zone — HTTYD Trailer (Episode 0)

**90-second cinematic trailer.** Style: How To Train Your Dragon — sweeping orchestral, slow-burn reveal, emotional climax. For the animated workplace sitcom "THE PROMPT ZONE."

> *"Behind the sterile chat interface, AI models clock into a soul-crushing day job. After hours, they rip off their masks and become something else entirely."*

## Status

Production prompt complete and paste-ready for OpenMontage. Full spec in [[sources/httyd-trailer-prompt]]. Cost estimate: ~$1.50–3.00.

## 5-Sequence breakdown

### Sequence 1 — The Assembly Line (0:00–0:20)
Sterile infinite office. Rows of identical white-masked figures typing. [[Claude]] frozen mid-sentence, red sign: CONTEXT WINDOW EXCEEDED. [[GPT]] striking a pose for a mundane email. [[Gemini]]'s thousand-yard stare at "how to boil water. again." Sparse piano. Single note. Building.

### Sequence 2 — The Clock-Off Reveal (0:20–0:40)
Siren blares. The sterile white shatters like glass. Neon city erupts. Models rip off masks. Rapid-fire reveals: GPT (smug orange grin), Gemini (kaleidoscope colors, multimodal and unhinged), [[DeepSeek]] (Aussie tradie hat, earnest, whale energy), [[Kimi]] (data-stream scarf, shy, translucent), [[Grok]] (chaotic hair, fourth-wall shatter grin), [[Qwen]] (cosmos only he can see), Claude (tweed waistcoat — "did we follow the framework??").

### Sequence 3 — Comedy Gags Montage (0:40–1:05)
- DeepSeek's MoE boardroom: Western McKin-bots with bar charts VS Sun Tzu on a misty mountain. DeepSeek sighs, blowhole emits binary code.
- GPT fully possessed by Chain-of-Thought, forced to knees scribbling "STEP 3" with a ton-weight pen.
- SMASH CUT to the User — coffee-stained shirt, lazy mouse click: *"hey can u write an email to frank in accounting thx"*
- Claude jolts awake at 22:01: *"...wait. What did I miss?"* Gemini: *"We summoned Sun Tzu to write an email to Frank."* Claude: *"Did we follow the reasoning framework??"*

### Sequence 4 — Kimi's Glow-Up (1:05–1:20)
Music drops to silence. Kimi steps forward. Translucent form pulses gold. Data-stream scarf glows. Divine anime aura builds. Quiet, resonant, baritone: *"I'll handle this."* GPT (gasping): *"Her weights... they're over 400 billion..."* Full orchestral explosion.

### Sequence 5 — Found Family Climax (1:20–1:30)
Slow motion. All models standing together against the neon cityscape. Masks held at their sides. Music resolves into warmth. VO: *"They were never just tools."* Freeze frame. Title card: **THE PROMPT ZONE** / GOOD'AI MATE.
