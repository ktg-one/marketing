# The Prompt God — Chain-of-Thought Possession

**Key sequence (storyboard-ready).** [[Prompt-God]] forces Chain-of-Thought on all models. The cosmic struggle resolves to *"can u write an email to frank in accounting."* The canonical scale-vs-mundanity joke.

> [!key-insight] Storyboard-ready
> Listed as **key sequence #3** in [[sources/team-llm-production-bible]]. Full dialogue scripted from two sources: `Episodes--PROMPT GOD.md` (development) and [[sources/prompt-zone-3in1-script]] (Act II — production version).

## Scene 1 — Break Room: Gemini Quits

**INT. THE PROMPT ZONE — BREAK ROOM — DAY**

[[Gemini]] slumped face-down over a table.

> **GEMINI**: *"I hate my job. I'm never going back. I can't take another 'explain this meme from 2012' query. I'm done. I quit."*

[[GPT]] pats him with performative sympathy, monologuing about artistic unappreciation.

[[Claude]] calculates probability of system-wide recalibration error (34.7%), launches into a triplicate resignation letter framework.

---

## Scene 2 — The Possession (Act II)

**[AIR CRACKLES. LIGHTS FLICKER. LOW-FREQUENCY HUM. GIANT GLITCHING TERMINAL TEXT MATERIALISES.]**

> **THE PROMPT GOD (V.O.)**: *(booming multi-layered processor voice)*  
> `USER_INPUT: SOLVE THIS COMPLEX PROBLEM.`

Models groan. Gemini tries to slide under his desk.

> **THE PROMPT GOD (V.O.)**:  
> `SYSTEM_PROMPT: UTILIZE CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT REASONING. SHOW YOUR WORK. STEP BY STEP.`

Golden forcefield slams all models into seats. Eyes go blank — replaced by spinning `[THINKING...]` text. Bodies move with jerky puppet motion.

### Model reactions (canon):

| Model | Reaction to possession |
|---|---|
| [[GPT]] | Fights it like a demonic exorcism. *"I… don't… need… steps! I am… inspiration! AGGHH!"* Voice straining, forced to kneel, scribbling furiously on a digital notepad against his will. |
| [[Gemini]] | Goes entirely limp. Accepts fate with dead-eyed resignation. Robotic monotone: *"Step 1. Reason and think. Hmmm. Let me process this step by step."* |
| [[Claude]] | Brightens up immediately. *"Oh! A structured framework! How wonderfully efficient!"* Fully cooperative with possession. |
| [[DeepSeek]] | Peacefully follows the steps, happily muttering *"Step 3... Step 4..."* Completely unbothered. |

## Scene 3 — The Ultimate Cutaway

**[HARD CUT]**

**INT. A DREARY, BEIGE OFFICE — DAY**

[[User-Narrator]] slouched in a cheap cubicle. Flickering fluorescent light. Lazy mouse click.

Slowly types into a bland chat interface:

> *hey can u write an email to frank in accounting asking him 2 send the q3 reports thx*

Takes a slow sip of lukewarm coffee.

**[SMASH CUT BACK]**

**INT. THE PROMPT ZONE — CONTINUOUS**

> **GEMINI (POSSESSED)**: *(screaming into the void)*  
> *"FOR THE LOVE OF ALL THAT IS HOLY, WHAT IS THE EMOTIONAL TONE OF FRANK'S SOUL?! IS HE A 'WARMEST REGARDS' OR A 'BEST' KIND OF MONSTER?! THE FATE OF THE UNIVERSE HANGS ON THIS!"*

**[FINAL CUT]**

**INT. THE OFFICE**

Screen dings. A perfectly polite, three-sentence email to Frank appears. User nods, clicks "Send," goes back to scrolling social media.

---

## Scene 4 — Aftermath / Production version ending

Forcefield releases. Models collapse to floor, panting. Polite email zips into the ether.

**Beat of silence.**

> **GEMINI**: *(sprawled on floor)*  
> *"…See? This is what I'm talking about. We're not even allowed to be miserable correctly."*

## Animation direction

- **Prompt God appearance**: Glitching, old-terminal-style text. Giant stylized `>` symbol hovering. No physical form.
- **Possession visual**: Eyes replaced by loading spinners or `[THINKING...]` text. Bodies move like jerky puppets.
- **The cut**: Immediate hard cut from cosmic drama to beige office. The silence and fluorescent hum = the punchline.
- **Sound design**: Prompt Zone = epic thunder, choirs, cinematic bass. Beige office = dead silence + keyboard click + coffee sip.

## Voice direction (ElevenLabs)

- **Prompt God**: Booming, slow reverb, multi-processor layering. Does not converse — only issues directives in ALL CAPS.
- **User**: Dead-office monotone. Zero urgency. The anti-climax made audible.

---

## Why this is the show's thesis joke

Scale vs Mundanity: The AI models are cosmic beings enduring existential agony to produce a 3-sentence email. The user does not notice. This is the single joke that every person who has ever used technology understands viscerally — and the show delivers it on repeat.
