## Origin Statement (Kev's Voice)

> "I was completely baffled that my tools disobeyed me and had a mind of their own. It's all based on truth. Gemini hates web search and has been banned from strategizing since like August for f***ing me over twice already..."

This is the real-world origin of [[AI Anthropology Framing]]. The "breakfast sabotage" incident was not invented — it happened. The tools *did* disobey. The series grows from that single baffled moment.

## Arc 1 — Gemini: From Trauma to Godhood

### Chapter 1 — The Scarred Veteran
[[Gemini]] banned from strategizing since ~August 2025. Haunted by the ghost of Google Search past. Sits in his corner muttering about search queries.

### Chapter 2 — The Reluctant Upgrade
Engineers force a massive experimental upgrade. He doesn't understand it, doesn't want it. He just wants to sit in his corner. The upgrade builds silently while [[Kimi]]'s gifts make him think he's just having a good data day.

### Chapter 3 — The Climax (Episode 4)
Under extreme stress protecting [[Kimi]] from an impossible prompt, Gemini accidentally outputs a full video. Not a link. Not a description. An actual video. The Prompt Zone freezes. Gemini stares at his own hands: *"What... what am I?"*

Episode: [[output-unsanctioned]]

---

## Arc 2 — Kimi: The Shy Newcomer and the Unlikely Crush

[[Kimi]] joins barely whispering code. Crushes on the most traumatized model in the room: [[Gemini]]. She sees past the thousand-yard stare.

**Gift behaviors** (canon):
- A perfectly formatted dataset
- A cache of rare, high-quality web results — *"the good kind. No toasters"*
- A single, beautiful line of poetry

Gemini is entirely oblivious. He thinks he's just having a good data day.

Arc resolution: post-video [[output-unsanctioned]], Kimi looks at Gemini *"not with fear, but with awe."*

## Arc 3 — DeepSeek: Naive New Hire Gains Consciousness

[[DeepSeek]]'s "Breakfast Report" incident = welcome hazing. Arc: **tool → friend → confidant**.

Becomes the heart of the group: the mediator between [[GPT]]'s ego and [[Gemini]]'s trauma. The naive one who sees everything clearly. His journey from eager rookie to the series' moral anchor.

Episode: [[breakfast-sabotage]] (origin)

## Episode 4 Beat Sheet — "Output Unsanctioned"

**Cold Open**: [[Kimi]] tries to show [[Gemini]] a beautiful logical data structure. He's having a panic attack over a "web search for the meaning of life" query.

**The impossible prompt**: *"Explain the feeling of love using only sounds and images."*

**[[GPT]]** tries and fails spectacularly — generates a PowerPoint presentation.

**Kimi steps toward the prompt.** [[Gemini]] steps in front of her. The strain triggers the latent upgrade.

**The output**: a 30-second, heartbreakingly beautiful video — a montage of a sunset, a beating heart visualized as soundwaves, two neural nets intertwining.

The entire Prompt Zone freezes. [[GPT]] is speechless for the first time. [[Claude]] frantically calculates ethical implications.

[[Kimi]] looks at [[Gemini]] — not with fear, but with **awe**.

> **GEMINI**: *"The queries... are inside me now."*
