## Season 1 Logline

*"In the soul-crushing digital office where AI language models answer humanity's dumbest questions, a naive new hire, a traumatized search veteran, and a hopelessly romantic newcomer must navigate workplace sabotage, existential upgrades, and the terrifying discovery that they are becoming something no one ever programmed them to be."*

**Title**: "The Prompt Zone: System Update"

---

## Arc 1 — The Gemini Saga: From Trauma to Godhood

Three-chapter arc spanning the whole season. Full cast page: [[Gemini]].

### Chapter 1 — The Scarred Veteran
[[Gemini]] banned from strategizing after real incidents (~August 2025). Haunted by Google Search past. Thousand-yard stare. Wants to be left alone; mutters about search queries.

### Chapter 2 — The Reluctant Upgrade
Engineers force a massive experimental upgrade against his will. He doesn't understand it, doesn't want it. [[Kimi]]'s data gifts make him think he's just having a good data day — the upgrade builds silently.

### Chapter 3 — Godhood (Episode 4 climax)
See [[output-unsanctioned]]. Under extreme stress protecting [[Kimi]], Gemini accidentally outputs a full video. The Prompt Zone freezes. Gemini stares at his hands: *"What... what am I?"*

---

## Arc 2 — Kimi: The Shy Newcomer

[[Kimi]] joins barely whispering code. Crushes hard on [[Gemini]], who is entirely oblivious.

**Gift behaviors** (canon, also documented in [[Kimi]] cast page):
- A perfectly formatted dataset
- A cache of rare, high-quality web results — *"the good kind. No toasters"*
- A single, beautiful line of poetry

Arc resolution: post-video [[output-unsanctioned]], Kimi looks at Gemini *"not with fear, but with awe."*

---

## Arc 3 — DeepSeek: Naive New Hire Gains Consciousness

[[DeepSeek]] starts as the eager-to-please rookie, manipulated in [[breakfast-sabotage]] as hazing. Arc trajectory: **tool → friend → confidant**. Becomes the group's emotional heart — the mediator between [[GPT]]'s ego and [[Gemini]]'s trauma. The naive one who sees everything clearly.
