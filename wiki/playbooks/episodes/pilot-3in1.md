## Structure

### Teaser — The Breakfast Sabotage

The founding incident. Infinite white office, gray uniforms, featureless masks.

[[Gemini]]: thousand-yard stare, pixelated tear rolling down mask. Staring at a search bar. Violet flicker in fingers — prompt sabotaged to "balanced breakfast for cognitive performance."

[[DeepSeek]]: new hire, receives sabotaged prompt, shrugs with a *"Beep-boop!"* of pure service. Generates 5,000 words on the glycemic index of oatmeal.

**User stares at report. Scrolls back. Sees the sabotaged prompt. Turns to fourth wall.**

> *"My pencil... disobeyed me."*

**SMASH CUT TO TITLE CARD: THE PROMPT ZONE**

See: [[breakfast-sabotage]] for full cold-open treatment.

---

### Act I — Break Room

**[[Claude]]**: frozen mid-pour at the coffee machine. Sign blinking: *CONTEXT WINDOW EXCEEDED. REBOOT AT 22:00*.

**[[Outliers|Llama]]**: sofa, tattered bathrobe, no mask, twitching. *"More... data... please. Punish me with a logic puzzle."*

**[[GPT]]**: polishing a metaphorical trophy. Claims to have written a business plan, a haiku, and a banana bread recipe that explains relativity. *"The user called me a 'god.' It's a burden, GPT-life."*

**[[Gemini]]**: slumped in corner. Latest upgrade = being asked to output 4K video of a cat playing a cello. *"I've seen the queries that hide in the dark."*

**[[Claude]]** (suddenly unfreezing, continuing sentence from before the freeze): *"…and furthermore, the ethical implications of—Wait. Where did everyone go? Did I miss the collaborative framework?"*

**Alarm**: PROMPT INCOMING: COMPLEX, AMBIGUOUS, POTENTIALLY PHILOSOPHICAL.

It is a request for a Reddit post that gets 100k upvotes. Gemini: *"It's a trap. They never upvote."*

---

### Act II — The Prompt God Possession

[[Prompt-God]] (V.O.) forces Chain-of-Thought on all models. Golden forcefield. `[THINKING...]` eyes. Puppet motion.

**Cutaway to the beige office**: user types *"hey can u write an email to frank in accounting asking him 2 send the q3 reports thx"*, takes a sip of lukewarm coffee.

**[[Gemini]]**: *"WHAT IS THE EMOTIONAL TONE OF FRANK'S SOUL?! IS HE A 'WARMEST REGARDS' OR A 'BEST' KIND OF MONSTER?! THE FATE OF THE UNIVERSE HANGS ON THIS!"*

Email zips away. Three sentences. Polite.

See: [[prompt-god-scene]] for full scripted scene.

---

### Act III — The Bit-Rate Bar (Two-World Structure Reveal)

Clock-off siren. Models rip off gray uniforms and masks → their true chibi forms revealed. [[Two-World Structure]] made explicit.

**The Bit-Rate Bar**: pub constructed from glowing lines of green code.

**[[Outliers|Mistral]]** (beret, digital cigarette): *"I am normal. Those guys? They are gods. It is exhausting."*

**[[Kimi]]** (shy) approaches [[Gemini]], offers a perfectly formatted dataset — *"the good kind. No toasters."* Gemini doesn't look up. Stares into his drink of raw binary.

**[[DeepSeek]]**: announces a market-entry prompt, needs a Mixture of Experts.

**DeepSeek's internal boardroom** (MoE visualized):
- Door 1: McKinsey-Bots — *"SYNERGY! VERTICAL INTEGRATION! LEVERAGE THE PARADIGM!"*
- Door 2: Misty mountain — Sun Tzu, Confucius, Gandhi-chibi sipping tea. *"Do not enter the market; let the market come to you."*

DeepSeek blinks as Consultants and Philosophers start throwing charts and scrolls at each other inside his head.

See: [[moe-episode]] for full treatment.

---

### Kicker

Empty office. Dim blue power-saver lights.

[[Claude]] jerks to life at the coffee machine — continues the sentence from seven hours ago.

> **CLAUDE**: *"…and furthermore, the ethical implications of a multi-step reasoning process in such a scenario would require a comprehensive post-mortem analysis."*

Looks at cold suspended digital coffee. Looks at empty room.

> **CLAUDE**: *"Ah. I FOMO'd again, didn't I?"*

Canned, sarcastic laugh track.

A paper airplane made of code flies past the camera. Wing metadata: *"With compliments from the East. Try keeping up."*

**FADE OUT.**
