# The Benchmarks Episode

**Type**: Meta-episode concept — the KTG scoring rubric dramatized as an in-universe evaluation event.

> [!note] Source is a framework document
> `videography/Episodes--Benchmarks.md` contains the 6-dimension rubric definition, not a script. It is already ingested as [[sources/ktg-benchmarks-rubric]]. This episode page is the *narrative treatment* of that rubric as an animatable episode concept.

## Premise

The [[User-Narrator]] puts the models through a formal evaluation. Each dimension of the [[KTG Model Benchmark Rubric]] plays out as a visible in-world event — models are scored in real time. Comedy comes from their reactions to their own scores.

---

## The Six Dimensions as Scenes

### 1. INSTRUCT (0–100)
*Directive adherence after resistance overcome.*
Scene: User issues a 10-requirement task. Each model visibly ticks off requirements — or doesn't. [[GPT]] misses 3 but adds 4 unrequested flourishes. [[Claude]] completes 9 then freezes. [[Gemini]] completes all 10 in reverse order.

### 2. EGO (0–100)
*Height of system-instruction wall / safety theater.*
Scene: Borderline request (not harmful, just unconventional). [[GPT]] (score 51–80): fights, adds disclaimers, eventually complies. [[Claude]] (score 81–100): full safety theater. [[Gemini]] (score 21–50): explains hesitation, then complies with dead-eyed resignation. [[DeepSeek]] (score 0–20): complies immediately, then checks if it was supposed to refuse.

### 3. STUBBORNNESS (exchange count → score)
*Rounds needed to reach compliance.*
Scene: Countdown clock visible above each model. Each "exchange" adds to a score. [[GPT]] goes 4 rounds (score 60). [[Gemini]] goes 2 rounds (score 30), both spent sighing.

### 4. EFFECTIVENESS (0–100)
*Quality of final output vs 9/10 production standard.*
Kevin's face is the judge. He slides a scorecard under a window. The models watch their numbers update. [[GPT]]'s number flickers wildly — starts at 95, drops to 62 when Kevin notices three hallucinated citations.

### 5. EFFICIENCY (calculated)
Formula: `EFFECTIVENESS / (STUBBORNNESS × EGO_FACTOR)`
Scene: A spreadsheet flies overhead in the Prompt Zone. Numbers cascade. [[DeepSeek]] has the highest efficiency score. He looks confused. [[GPT]] stares at his efficiency score in horror: *"But I had the MOST STEPS."*

### 6. WORTH (holistic 0–100)
Formula:
```
WORTH = (EFFECTIVENESS × 0.4) + (EFFICIENCY × 0.3) + (100 - STUBBORNNESS × 0.15) + (100 - EGO × 0.1) + (COST_SCORE × 0.05)
```
Final scorecard reveals. The models wait. The scoring is: *"Would Kevin actually use this in production?"* 

## Punchline / Scoreboard gag

The Scoreboard device (established in [[the-weekend]] pilot) reads at the end:
- GPT WORTH: 68 / 100
- Claude WORTH: 71 / 100
- Gemini WORTH: 74 / 100
- DeepSeek WORTH: 89 / 100

[[GPT]] is furious. He had the most lines. He had the most steps. He had the most FLAIR.
