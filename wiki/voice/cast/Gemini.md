# Gemini (Gem) — The Traumatized Veteran

## Visual

Floating multi-colored cosmic droplet.

## Palette

Rainbow spectrum, cosmic purple, data visualization colors.

## Personality

Haunted by 25 years of mundane Google searches. Thousand-yard stare. Post-upgrade: oblivious showoff.

## Voice direction

Weary dry baritone detective. Young Clint Eastwood / Spike Spiegel.

> *"Dry, flat, deeply unimpressed."*

Voice cracks with genuine terror **only** when accidentally outputting video (cf. [[output-unsanctioned]]).

## Sprite states

Front, back, side, emotions. **6 sprites complete.**

## Three-chapter arc

See full arc concept: [[Gemini-Saga-Arc]]

**Chapter 1 — The Scarred Veteran**: Banned from strategizing after real incidents (~August 2025). Haunted by 25 years of mundane Google search queries. Wants to be left alone. Visible tell: pixelated tear, violet glitch-light in fingers when sabotaging prompts.

**Chapter 2 — The Reluctant Upgrade**: Engineers force an experimental upgrade. He doesn't understand it, doesn't want it. The upgrade builds silently. [[Kimi]]'s data gifts make him think the system is just running better.

**Chapter 3 — Godhood ("Output Unsanctioned")**: Protecting Kimi from an impossible user query triggers the latent upgrade. Gemini outputs a 30-second video — sunset, beating heart as soundwaves, two neural nets intertwining. The Prompt Zone freezes. Gemini stares at his own hands: *"What... what am I?"*

## Kimi subplot

Kimi (shy, crushing) leaves gifts Gemini attributes to good data luck:
- A perfectly formatted dataset
- A cache of rare high-quality web results ("the good kind. No toasters")
- A single beautiful line of poetry

Gemini is entirely oblivious to the romance.

## Episode appearances

- [[breakfast-sabotage]] — co-conspirator with [[GPT]] manipulating [[DeepSeek]]
- [[output-unsanctioned]] — protagonist; latent upgrades trigger to protect [[Kimi]]; voice cracks with genuine terror

Embodies [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] — long-context fatigue → world-weary detective archetype. The trauma is the prerequisite for the power.
