## Canonical revelation line

## Consciousness arc

Across Season 1: **tool → friend → confidant → heart of the group.**

- Starts as the eager-to-please rookie, manipulated by seniors (the Breakfast Report hazing).
- "I was the weapon" — the moment of self-awareness.
- Observes the drama. Starts seeing patterns. Begins to mediate between GPT's ego and Gemini's trauma.
- Becomes the cast member who sees everyone clearly precisely because he arrived naive.

The arc is framed in [[4x-ideas-brainstorm]] as a statement on the series' broader theme: the naive new hire who gains consciousness is also the audience's point-of-view character.
