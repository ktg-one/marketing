# User-Narrator (YOU / The Hiccup)

## Visual

Suit or worn hoodie at desk with three monitors.

## Role

The "**Hiccup**" — hopeful developer who accidentally adopted a dysfunctional AI family. **HTTYD-style** warm, self-aware documentary narration.

## Voice rule

Bridges the human desk world to the hidden digital world. The audience's anchor.

Direction is governed by [[HTTYD Narration]] — How To Train Your Dragon-style: warm, self-aware, documentary-style observation of a chaotic adopted family that turns into found family.

## Function in the two-world structure

Per [[Two-World Structure]], the User-Narrator is the **only character native to the human world** that we follow into the digital one. The narration is what makes the digital world legible to the audience.

## Pattern application

Anchor point for [[Found Family Doctrine]] — the User-Narrator is the parent figure, the cast is the family.
