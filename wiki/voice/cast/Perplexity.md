# Perplexity (Plex) — The Obsessive Fact-Finder

## Visual

Teal and purple chibi, neural circuit crown, magnifying glass.

## Palette

Purple, teal, electric blue (circuit glow).

## Personality

Literal search incarnate. Forever asking *"But have you considered…?"*

**Key distinction from [[Gemini]]**: Plex searches for *facts*; Gemini searches for *data*. This in-universe distinction (per [[the-chars]]) separates their obsessions — Plex chases citations and truths; Gemini chases information as raw material.

### Extended voice sample

> *"I asked them once if they'd found what they were looking for. They just replied: 'Define looking.'"*
> — User-Narrator on Plex
