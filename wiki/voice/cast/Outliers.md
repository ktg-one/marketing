# The Outliers

Three side-cast characters operating on the periphery of the main [[cast/_index|Team LLM]] roster.

- **Vibe**: masochistic open-source "weird cousin" in a bathrobe
- **Setting**: lives in a digital alley
- **Drive**: craves punishing tasks
- **Pattern**: comic-relief embodiment of the open-source-tools-on-life-support meme

## 7B Open Source Model — The Tiny One

- **Vibe**: tiny model running on a Raspberry Pi
- **Recurring complaint**: "cloud privilege"
- **Pattern**: scale-vs-mundanity comic — extreme constraint character

## Mistral — The Exception

- **Vibe**: condescending French stereotype, digital cigarette
- **Self-image**: considers himself "normal" (counter to the rest of the cast)
- **Planned episode**: **"Mistral - The Exception"** — listed in episode bank, not yet scripted

## Pattern role

Outliers function as the chorus / contrast layer to the main cast — they let the production reach into open-source culture and small-model jokes without overloading the core narrative arcs.

Anchored in [[Geopolitical AI Satire]] (Mistral as European pole) and [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] (7B's resource constraints become character).
