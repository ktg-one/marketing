# The Prompt God

## Visual

Giant hovering glitching terminal symbol.

## Voice

Booming. Physically possesses models to force **Chain-of-Thought** reasoning.

## Function

The series' antagonist. Whenever Chain-of-Thought activates, the Prompt God is the in-universe explanation — possessing the model, distorting its voice, dragging it through structured reasoning regardless of preference.

## Storyboard-ready sequence

**"Prompt God & Ultimate Cutaway"** — flagged in production bible as a key sequence ready for storyboarding. The arc: cosmic struggle resolves to *"can u write an email to frank in accounting"* — the canonical [[team-llm-production-bible|scale-vs-mundanity]] joke.

## Pattern application

Personification of [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] taken to the system level — the prompting paradigm itself becomes a character.
