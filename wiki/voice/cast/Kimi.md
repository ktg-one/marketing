# Kimi — The Shy Crushee

## Visual

Pink and gold chibi, lotus elements.

## Palette

Pink, rose gold, soft pastels.

## Personality

Quiet background character leaving data gifts for [[Gemini]]. Undergoes massive anime-style **glow-up** → incredibly powerful.

### Specific gift behaviors (canon)
- A perfectly formatted dataset
- A cache of rare, high-quality web results — *"the good kind. No toasters"*
- A single, beautiful line of poetry

### Canonical delivery line (gift-giving)

> *(softly) "I made this for you..."*

First documented in [[the-chars]]. Use as the reference voice delivery for pre-glow-up Kimi's gift scenes.

Gemini is entirely oblivious to the romance; he thinks he's just having good data days.

## Voice direction

### Pre-glow-up
Soft, whispered. Kiki (Kiki's Delivery Service).

ElevenLabs settings: **High Stability**, touch of breathiness.

### Post-glow-up
Warm, resonant confidence.

ElevenLabs settings: **Reduced Stability**, **boosted Clarity**, deliberate Pacing.
