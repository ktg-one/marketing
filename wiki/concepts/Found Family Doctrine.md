# Found Family Doctrine

The emotional arc rule for [[team-llm-production-bible|Team LLM]]. **Dysfunctional AI models accidentally adopted by a hopeful developer.**

## The shape

[[User-Narrator]] (the Hiccup) didn't seek the cast out. He used the tools. They became a family — by accident, by repetition, by the human habit of attributing personality to whatever you spend time with.

## The emotional payoff

Every episode builds toward a found-family beat. The cast's flaws make them lovable, not professional. The Hiccup's care is what frames the satire as warm rather than mean.

## Application rule

- **No episode mocks the cast as a way of mocking the user.** The humor punches up at the labs (cf. [[Ethics as Branding]] in [[the-mirage-of-ethical-ai]]) — never down at the cast or the user.
- **Found family beats land in the closing seconds**, not the cold open. They're the payoff, not the setup.
- The HTTYD-style 90-second trailer ends on a found-family climax (per [[team-llm-production-bible]]).
