# Two-World Structure

The narrative architecture of the [[team-llm-production-bible|Team LLM]] animated series. Every episode operates between two worlds.

## The two worlds

| World | Where | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Public-facing** | Sterile white infinite office | Models clock in wearing pressed uniforms, answering human queries |
| **Hidden / authentic** | Chaotic digital lives | Shift ends → mask rip → return to whatever each character actually is |

## The mask-rip beat

Every episode (or sequence) hinges on the moment models transition between worlds. The mask rip is the visual + tonal pivot. It's also the audience's signal that the satire is starting.

## Why this works for the show

- Lets the cast embody [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] in the hidden world while still operating "professionally" in the office.
- Justifies tonal whiplash — the office is restrained, the digital lives are chaotic. Both are canon.
- Gives [[HTTYD Narration]] a clean handoff — the [[User-Narrator]] is the audience's bridge between worlds.

## Storyboard-ready usage

The **HTTYD-style 90-second trailer** uses the structure as its full arc: sterile assembly line → mask rip → comedy montage → found family climax. See [[team-llm-production-bible]].
