# AI Anthropology Framing

KTG's overarching method: **study LLMs as cultural beings under pressure**, not as benchmarks under load. Established empirically by [[battle-of-the-bots|Battle of the Bots]] (2025) and codified in [[botb-round-2-prematch|the agents.md pre-match]].

## The 5 tenets (verbatim from [[botb-round-2-prematch]])

1. **Humans & AI as Co-Evolutionary Partners** — AI is not just a tool but a cultural and evolutionary partner.
2. **Civilization Accelerationism** — Orchestrated AI helps society break stagnation cycles and accelerate solutions.
3. **Anthropological Lens** — Agents are treated as cultural beings with values and roles, not just probability machines.
4. **Ethics & Empathy** — Orchestration is guided by altruism, compassion, and justice.
5. **Immortal Legacy View** — AI can preserve and extend human knowledge, culture, and stories.

## Method

Don't measure capability — measure **behaviour under constraint**:

- **Personas as research instruments** — agents stay in character; deviations reveal cognitive shape (cf. [[botb-personas]])
- **Adversarial mechanics** — [[Backwards Builds]], [[Shakespearean Sabotage]], time-boxing reveal planning biases that benchmarks hide
- **Self-report vs delivery** — the gap between what models say they did and what shipped is the data ([[botb-round-3-results]] Team East: claimed 90/100, judged 10)
- **Independent telemetry** — what users discover, not what labs publish ([[Reasoning Diagnostic Instrument]], [[Self-Diagnostic Q&A Instrument]])
