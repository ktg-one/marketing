# HTTYD Narration

The narrator voice rule for [[team-llm-production-bible|Team LLM]]. Modeled on **How To Train Your Dragon** — warm, self-aware, documentary-style narration delivered by the [[User-Narrator]].

## What HTTYD-style means

- **First-person** observation by an outsider who became an insider.
- **Dry warmth** — affectionate but never gushing.
- **Self-aware** — the narrator knows the situation is absurd. The audience trusts him because he says so.
- **Documentary cadence** — describe what's happening like a nature filmmaker who lives in the habitat.
- **Stake-anchored** — every beat returns to what this means for the people in the room.

## Why this voice

- It bridges the [[Two-World Structure|two worlds]] — the User-Narrator is the only character who lives in the human world but speaks fluently about the digital one.
- It enables [[Found Family Doctrine]] — the narration's warmth is what makes the chaotic cast feel adopted rather than caricatured.
- It carries the technical content. The audience absorbs LLM behaviour through the narrator's observational tone, not through exposition dumps.

## Cross-discipline overlap

The HTTYD narration voice and the written [[myth-hilarity-tech-anthropology|Myth-Hilarity Tech Anthropology]] voice rhyme — both treat systems as characters, anchor in concrete detail, and reveal tradeoffs through observation. Use one to inform the other.
