# Geopolitical AI Satire

The AI race recast as office politics. **West = arrogant corporate. East = philosophical or disruptive open-source.** A core production pillar of [[team-llm-production-bible|Team LLM]].

## The two poles

### West (corporate)
- [[GPT]] — narcissistic star
- [[Claude]] — anxious bureaucrat
- [[Gemini]] — traumatized veteran
- [[Outliers|Mistral]] — condescending European exception

Vibe: pressed uniforms, sterile office, ego, defensive escalation. Embodied at scale via the [[team-llm-production-bible|McKin-bot consultant boardroom]] inside [[DeepSeek]]'s mind.

### East (philosophical / disruptive)
- [[DeepSeek]] — naive earnest newcomer with Sun Tzu / Confucius / Gandhi mental boardroom
- [[Kimi]] — quiet → glow-up arc
- [[Qwen]] — cosmic stargazer dropping prophetic one-liners

Vibe: misty mountain temple, philosophical depth, asymmetric disruption (the open-source paper-airplane drop in [[cognitive-overclock]]).

## The narrative function

The East/West frame lets the show comment on real industry tension (DeepSeek's open-source release upending the proprietary cycle, Anthropic's compute crisis enabling open-source competitors) without naming names directly. See [[Chibi Copyright Evasion]].

## Storyboard-ready application

[[moe-episode]] — Consultants vs Philosophers. Inside [[DeepSeek]]'s head: the West (McKin-bot consultants) and East (Sun Tzu et al.) literally sharing a strategy session.
