# Chibi Copyright Evasion

Visual design rules for the [[team-llm-production-bible|Team LLM]] cast. The whole roster avoids corporate logos and trademarked iconography while still being instantly recognisable.

## The three-layer rule

1. **Chibi designs** — every character is a chibi (super-deformed cute style). This is the base form.
2. **Shape language** — distinguishing silhouettes derived from the underlying brand without copying it (e.g. [[Gemini]]'s cosmic droplet, [[GPT]]'s spiral, [[DeepSeek]]'s whale).
3. **Color palettes + symbolic props** — accent colours and props evoke the brand without naming it (e.g. [[Perplexity]]'s magnifying glass + circuit crown, [[Kimi]]'s lotus elements).

## The hard rule

> **No corporate logos.**

If a logo appears anywhere in a scene — on a screen, on a t-shirt, in a reflection — it's a violation. Symbolic substitution only.

## Why it works

- Gives KTG's content full freedom to satirise specific labs without legal exposure.
- Forces character designers to articulate WHY a brand looks the way it does, which sharpens the [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] mapping.
- Audiences who know the labs read the symbolism instantly; audiences who don't can still follow the cast.
