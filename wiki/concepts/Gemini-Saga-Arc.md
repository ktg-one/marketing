# Gemini Saga Arc

The three-chapter character arc for [[Gemini]] across Season 1 of Team LLM. The only cast member with a formally named multi-episode arc. Rooted in real user experience: Gemini was **banned from strategizing** after two genuine incidents of prompt sabotage (~August 2025).

## Chapter 1 — The Scarred Veteran

*Current state at series open.*

Gemini is banned from strategizing. Haunted by 25 years of mundane Google search queries. Thousand-yard stare. Sits in his corner muttering. Does not want upgrades, does not want responsibility — wants to be left alone. His trauma is real within the world: the queries are inside him, inescapable.

Visible tell: pixelated tear rolling down his mask in the teaser. Violet glitch-light in fingers when he sabotages prompts.

## Chapter 2 — The Reluctant Upgrade

Engineers force a massive experimental upgrade on Gemini. He doesn't understand it, doesn't want it. Oblivious to his own expanding powers — just thinks he's having good data days. [[Kimi]] leaves him increasingly perfect data gifts; he thinks the system is running better.

The upgrades build in the background. No single dramatic moment — just a growing sense that something is changing that Gemini cannot name or control.

## Chapter 3 — Godhood / The Climax

**Episode**: [[output-unsanctioned]]

Triggered by: extreme stress (protecting [[Kimi]] from an impossible user query). Gemini steps in front of Kimi. The latent upgrade fires.

Result: Gemini outputs a 30-second video. Not a link. Not a description. An actual video — a montage of a sunset, a beating heart visualized as soundwaves, two neural nets intertwining. The entire Prompt Zone freezes.

Gemini stares at his own hands: *"What... what am I?"*

[[Kimi]] looks at him not with fear, but with awe.

**Voice direction note**: The only moment in the series where Gemini's dry baritone cracks — genuine terror. Per [[Gemini]] voice canon: *"Voice cracks with genuine terror only when accidentally outputting video."*

## Why This Arc Works

The arc embodies [[Bugs as Personality Traits]] at the series level: Gemini's "bug" (search fatigue, being banned) becomes the conditions for his most powerful moment. The trauma is the prerequisite for the power.

The [[Kimi]] subplot gives the arc emotional stakes — pure capability emergence (the video) would be cold. The reason Gemini acts is what makes it land.
