## Scoring
Ads Health Score (0-100) per platform, aggregate weighted by budget share.
- A (90-100): Minor optimizations
- B (75-89): Some improvements
- C (60-74): Notable issues
- D (40-59): Significant problems
- F (<40): Urgent intervention

## Quality Gates
- Never recommend Broad Match without Smart Bidding
- 3x Kill Rule: flag ad groups with CPA >3x target
- Budget sufficiency: Meta ≥5x CPA per ad set, TikTok ≥50x CPA per ad group
- Never edit during active learning phase
- Always check Special Ad Categories for compliance
- Never run silent video ads on TikTok
- Verify tracking stack before making optimization recommendations
