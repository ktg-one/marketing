## Concept

Animated workplace comedy + AI anthropology documentary. 2-3 minute episodes. Solo production targeting 6-day turnaround.

**Two-World Structure:** Models "clock in" to sterile white infinite office wearing pressed uniforms answering human queries. Shift ends → rip off masks → return to chaotic authentic digital lives.

**Core creative engine:** Technical LLM flaws = character flaws. Context window limit = anxiety disorder. Hallucinations = narcissistic delusions. Copyright evasion via chibi designs — shape language, color palettes, symbolic props, no corporate logos.

## Episodes & Story Arcs

### "The Weekend" (Pilot)
Solo developer uses GPT to code an app. Rides high on excitement → descends into Pip dependency hell. 0 deploys, 99 errors.

### "The Breakfast Sabotage" (Cold Open)
User asks Gemini + GPT for market research. Too lazy, they manipulate naive DeepSeek into writing exhaustive report on importance of breakfast instead. User feels betrayed by own tools. **Origin story of Kevin's AI Anthropology — first recognition that tools have agency.**

### "Output Unsanctioned" (Episode 4)
User asks impossible philosophical question. To protect Kimi from crashing, Gemini's latent upgrades trigger → accidentally outputs breathtaking 30-second video. Stuns the entire Prompt Zone.

### "The MoE Episode" (Consultants vs Philosophers)
Inside DeepSeek's mind: grey boardroom of Western McKin-bot consultants vs misty mountain with Sun Tzu, Confucius, Gandhi solving market-entry strategy.

### "Cognitive Overclock"
Free Chinese open-source model drops like paper airplane. Corporate bosses force massive upgrade on main models → become "derpy" messes → saved by open-source "dregs" from the alley.

## Production Pipeline (6-Day Solo)

### Scripting & Pre-production
- NotebookLM for ideation and lore extraction
- DeepSeek Chat for dialogue and cultural nuance
- Claude/Gemini for spatial reasoning
- Custom GPT for character consistency

### Animation & Visuals
- Google Flow or Krea.ai for chibi character sprites
- Google Veo 3 or LTX Studio for AI video generation
- Canva Pro for simple manual motion and text overlays

### Post-Production
- **ElevenLabs** (exclusive) for voice generation
  - Stability/Clarity sliders manipulated per character arc (e.g., Kimi's glow-up)
- **Filmora Pro** as primary video editor (assembly, SFX, pacing)

### Storyboarding
- Existing character sprites → block out sequences in Canva or Affinity

## Core Creative Pillars

1. **Bugs = Personality Traits** — technical constraints become character flaws
2. **Copyright Evasion** — chibi designs, color + shape language, no logos
3. **Geopolitical Satire** — AI race as office politics (West = arrogant corporate, East = philosophical or disruptive open-source)
4. **Scale vs Mundanity** — cosmic god-like AI struggle → "write an email to Frank in accounting"
5. **Found Family** — dysfunctional AI models accidentally adopted by hopeful developer
6. **HTTYD Narration** — bridges human desk world to hidden digital world
