EPISODE 1: "The Weekend" (Your True Pilot)
LOGLINE: A solo dev discovers ChatGPT and believes they can finally make an app. One weekend, 99 dependency errors, and zero working deploys later, they learn a painful truth: AI can't save you from Python.

ACT 1: HOPE (Friday Evening - "I Can Make An App!")
SCENE 1: Your Workspace - Friday, 5:30 PM

[You RUSH through the door. Jacket still on. You don't even sit down properly—you're leaning over your keyboard, hands hovering.]

YOU (breathless, excited): "Okay. Okay. ChatGPT. Let's do this."

[You open ChatGPT. The interface is pristine. Empty. Waiting.]

YOU (typing): "Write me a Python Flask app that does [X]."

[GPT's chibi appears on the monitor—confident, smiling, ready.]

GPT (voice-over, to himself): "Ah. A believer. They always start with Flask."

[GPT generates code. PAGES of it. You COPY-PASTE it into VSCode.]

YOU (whispering): "It's... it's actual code. This is going to work."

GPT (chibi doing a little bow): "Of course it's going to work. I am very good at this."

[You hit RUN. The terminal loads. No errors. The app STARTS.]

YOU (jumping up): "IT WORKS! OH MY GOD, IT WORKS!"

[Wide shot: Your desk. The single monitor glowing. You're dancing. GPT's chibi is also dancing on the screen.]

GPT (triumphant): "We did it! I mean—I did it. But we can share the credit."

[FREEZE FRAME on your joyful face.]

TITLE CARD: "THE WEEKEND"

[Time-stamp appears: Friday, 5:47 PM. 47 hours until Monday.]

ACT 2: DISASTER (Saturday - The Pip Dependency Hell)
SCENE 2: Your Workspace - Saturday Morning, 8:00 AM

[You're back at the desk. Coffee in hand. Determined. You're ready to DEPLOY.]

YOU (confident): "Okay, let's get this on a server. ChatGPT, how do I deploy a Flask app?"

[GPT generates deployment instructions. You follow them. Terminal commands. Pip installs.]

[ERROR MESSAGE appears.]

TERMINAL: ERROR: Could not find a version that satisfies the requirement

YOU (frowning): "Uh. Okay. Weird. Let me try again."

[You try again. Another error. Different dependency.]

GPT (chibi looking nervous): "Ah. Yes. Sometimes there are... version conflicts. But I can fix this. Just—"

YOU (reading GPT's new code): "Okay, try this version instead."

[10 MINUTES LATER: More errors. 20 MINUTES LATER: More errors.]

[MONTAGE: Error messages PILE UP. Your coffee goes cold. You're Googling. Stack Overflow tabs multiply. GPT is generating new suggestions. None work.]

[Time-stamp: Saturday, 2:17 PM]

YOU (frustrated, talking to the screen): "Why isn't this working?! You SAID it would work!"

GPT (defensive): "It DOES work! In a theoretical, context-free environment! Your system is... non-standard!"

YOU: "It's a Mac!"

GPT: "...Non-standard."

SCENE 3: Your Workspace - Saturday Evening, 9:43 PM

[You're slumped in your chair. Hair disheveled. The desk is a MESS—notebooks, Post-its, empty snack wrappers.]

[The terminal shows ERROR #87.]

YOU (dead voice): "Eighty-seven. That's eighty-seven errors."

GPT (chibi also looking exhausted): "I... I don't understand. The code is correct. The logic is sound. The issue must be... environmental."

YOU: "It's ALWAYS environmental! Every single time!"

[You close the laptop. Stand up. Walk away.]

GPT (calling after you): "Wait—I can try a different package manager! Have you considered Docker? Hello?"

[The screen goes dark. GPT's chibi sits alone in the void.]

GPT (to himself, quiet): "...Docker usually works."

ACT 3: DEFEAT (Sunday Night - "We Are Not Ready")
SCENE 4: Your Workspace - Sunday, 11:47 PM

[You're back. One last attempt. The determination is gone. This is just... grim obligation.]

YOU (flat voice, typing): "ChatGPT. One more time. Simple app. Minimal dependencies."

GPT (chibi reappears, trying to be cheerful): "Yes! Simple! I can do simple! Let's use Django—"

YOU: "You said Flask was better."

GPT: "I was... wrong. Django has built-in features that might—"

YOU: "Just. Give me. The code."

[GPT generates. You copy-paste. You run pip install.]

[99 packages to install. The progress bar CRAWLS.]

[You watch. GPT watches. Neither speaks.]

[Finally, the installs finish. You run the app.]

TERMINAL: ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'werkzeug'

[Beat. Long silence.]

YOU (whispering): "We installed that. I watched it install."

GPT (confused): "I... I don't know what's happening."

[You close the laptop. Put your head on the desk.]

YOU (muffled): "I thought I could make an app."

[Beat.]

YOU: "I can't make an app."

[The screen shows: Sunday, 11:58 PM. Two minutes until Monday.]

[The camera pans to the monitor. GPT's chibi is frozen—not from context limits, but from... something else. Guilt? Helplessness?]

GPT (voice-over, to himself): "I failed them. On their first weekend. I... I failed."

EPILOGUE: The Scoreboard
[Monday morning. Your desk. You're getting ready for work. The laptop is closed.]

[A POST-IT NOTE is stuck to the monitor. It reads:]

"Human & ChatGPT: 0. Pip Dependencies: 99."

[You grab your bag. Leave.]

[The monitor flickers on. GPT's chibi appears.]

GPT (to the empty room): "...I'll do better. Next time."

[FADE TO BLACK.]

END OF EPISODE 1.

Why This Episode is Perfect
1. Universal Relatability
Every developer who's tried to deploy ANYTHING has lived this nightmare. It's not about AI—it's about the crushing reality of software development that AI can't fix.

2. Character Introduction
You: The hopeful dreamer turned broken shell. We FEEL your pain.

GPT: Confident → Defensive → Helpless. His arc mirrors yours.

3. Emotional Stakes
This isn't about sabotage or jokes. This is heartbreak. You believed. GPT believed. Neither of you were ready.

4. The Setup for Episode 2 (Claude Arrives)
After this disaster, you'll try Claude. "Maybe he's better at code." Spoiler: He's better at explaining code, but he runs out of context before finishing.
