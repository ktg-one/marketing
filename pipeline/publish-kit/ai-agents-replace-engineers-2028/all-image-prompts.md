## 1. Hero Image (16:9) — Blog Header

**Use**: `Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental` or image gen model

```
Create a bold editorial illustration for a blog post about AI replacing software engineers by 2028.

STYLE: Dark background with neon accent lines, abstract digital art, futuristic tech aesthetic

COMPOSITION: 
- Silhouette of a software engineer at a laptop on the left side
- The silhouette is fading/transforming into circuit board patterns and glowing neural network nodes
- Emerging from the right side: abstract AI agent nodes, geometric shapes, bright cyan glow
- Transition zone in the middle showing the transformation

COLORS:
- Deep navy background (#0a1628)
- Electric cyan accents (#00d4ff) 
- Warm coral highlights (#ff6b6b)

TEXT: "2028" large, "The End of Engineering?" smaller
MOOD: Provocative, forward-looking, slightly unsettling
ASPECT RATIO: 16:9
```
